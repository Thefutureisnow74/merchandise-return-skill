# Intake Questions (about you and your item)

Your case details. Every question here is about **you and your own item** — the skill collects
**only your information, never anyone else's**. This is your return, in your own name. Identity and
jurisdiction come from your User Profile (§0 of SKILL.md); this set is about the specific item and
dispute.

**Rules of use**
1. Every field is 🔴 **BLOCKING** or 🟡 **parallel**. The case cannot leave CASE FILE (Phase 2)
   until every 🔴 is answered.
2. Record answers as comments on your Multica issue.
3. Ask plain, numbered questions — don't dump the table.
4. Missing paperwork is usually recoverable (serial number, card/order lookup) — capture what's on
   hand instead of stalling.

## Section 1 — The purchase
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Where** did you buy it? (retailer, marketplace, manufacturer-direct, carrier) | 🔴 | Decides who Tier 1 targets; retailer vs manufacturer are different ladders |
| 1.2 | Purchase date (approximate OK) | 🔴 | Drives every deadline: warranty, chargeback, statute of limitations |
| 1.3 | Exact amount paid (incl. tax) | 🔴 | Sets whether small claims is worth it and which court tier |
| 1.4 | **How did you pay?** card (which issuer) / PayPal / financing or installment / cash / gift card | 🔴 | Card or financing = a possible chargeback/CFPB path; note the ~120-day chargeback window; cash/gift card = none |
| 1.5 | Proof of purchase? receipt / order email / account order history | 🟡 | Helpful, **not fatal** — retailer lookup by name/card and the serial number substitute |
| 1.6 | In-store or online? | 🟡 | Online often carries stronger statutory return rights |

## Section 2 — The product
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Brand + **exact model** (size/capacity/variant) | 🔴 | Generic descriptions get rejected |
| 2.2 | Serial number / IMEI / VIN | 🔴 | **Often substitutes for a lost receipt** — the maker can look up sale + build date |
| 2.3 | New, open-box, refurbished, or used? | 🔴 | Materially changes what warranty applies |
| 2.4 | Extended warranty or protection plan? who issued it? | 🟡 | A separate, sometimes faster recovery path — **for a phone, a Protection 360 / Assurant plan may be the fastest route** |

## Section 3 — The problem
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Describe what's wrong, in your own words | 🔴 | Becomes the complaint narrative — preserve verbatim |
| 3.2 | **Defective from the start, or did it fail later?** | 🔴 | **The single most valuable question.** "From the start" = never conformed at sale → implied warranty of merchantability, survives an expired maker warranty. "Failed later" = weaker, warranty-dependent |
| 3.3 | When did you first notice it? | 🔴 | Timeline + whether reported promptly |
| 3.4 | Constant, intermittent, or specific conditions? | 🟡 | Intermittent faults need more documentation to survive inspection |
| 3.5 | Ever dropped, exposed to liquid, repaired, or modified? | 🔴 | **Ask directly and early.** The vendor will raise it — better to know now than be blindsided at Tier 2 |
| 3.6 | Currently usable at all? | 🟡 | Total failure vs degraded changes the remedy argument |

## Section 4 — What's already been tried
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | What troubleshooting have you done? | 🔴 | Pre-empts "did you restart it"; shows you did your part |
| 4.2 | Ruled out external causes? (network, power, accessories, other devices) | 🟡 | Strong isolating evidence |
| 4.3 | Already contacted the seller/manufacturer? | 🔴 | Decides which phase we enter — if already refused, we may start above Tier 1 |
| 4.4 | Existing case / ticket / RMA / claim numbers? | 🔴 | Never open a duplicate track — always reference the existing number |
| 4.5 | Names of anyone you spoke with + what they promised | 🟡 | A named rep's promise is leverage |
| 4.6 | **Did you report it to anyone at the time it failed?** (store, rep, chat, phone, email) — who, roughly when, and what did they say? | 🔴 | **Timely notice.** "I told the store and they didn't help" converts a stale-looking claim into one where the vendor was on notice from day one and simply failed to act |
| 4.7 | **If time has passed since then — what happened in between? What made you stop pushing?** | 🔴 | See §1.6. Ask WITHOUT judgment; there is no wrong answer. Discouragement, illness, moving, not knowing you could act — each maps to a real argument (vendor-caused delay, equitable tolling, the discovery rule). Record verbatim to `MR Delay Explanation` |

> **How to ask 4.6 / 4.7 — tone matters.** These are not a test of whether the user "deserves" the
> claim, and must never read that way. Say plainly: *"There's no wrong answer — I'm asking because
> the reason helps your case, not because it counts against you."* **Never** respond to an answer
> with any version of "that's outside the warranty / return window / statute, so there's nothing to
> do." This skill does not police the user out of their own claim (SKILL.md §1.6). An expired
> warranty is not an expired obligation: goods that failed after a handful of ordinary uses were
> never merchantable, and that argument outlives any manufacturer's 12-month card.

## Section 5 — Desired outcome
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | What do you want — refund, replacement, repair, or store credit? **Rank if more than one.** | 🔴 | The whole ladder is built backward from the ask; never guess. Matches the board's `MR Remedy Type` options (Refund/Replacement/Repair/StoreCredit) exactly — a vague "credit" doesn't tell `refund_landed` what to verify before the case can close (store credit requires a real code/card/certificate, not just a promise) |
| 5.2 | Any outcome you would NOT accept? | 🟡 | Prevents "winning" store credit they don't want |
| 5.3 | Willing to escalate to regulators and, if needed, small claims? | 🔴 | **Ask up front.** If no, the ladder terminates at Tier 2 and everyone knows on day one |
| 5.4 | A deadline or event that matters to you? | 🟡 | Affects pacing |

## Section 6 — Evidence
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | Photos or video of the defect | 🔴 | Especially for intermittent/visual faults |
| 6.2 | Screenshots of prior correspondence | 🟡 | Establishes the paper trail |
| 6.3 | The receipt / order confirmation if you have it | 🟡 | See 1.5 — missing is recoverable |

## Section 7 — Discrimination check
| # | Question | Priority | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | **Do you believe you were treated differently or unfairly because of your race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or another protected characteristic?** If yes — what specifically was said or done, by whom, and when? | 🔴 | Capture the **FACTS**, never manufacture a claim. A yes with supporting facts unlocks the civil-rights track (Tier 3-D): a separate statutory claim (e.g. CA Unruh, $4,000 statutory minimum), a civil-rights agency, and advocacy pressure — run in parallel, not after the consumer ladder |

## Tone for the outbound intake ask
- Plain numbered questions, not the table.
- Be honest about who's writing.
- Missing paperwork is usually recoverable — say so, so they don't stall.
- Close by telling them exactly what happens next and when they'll have their case number.
